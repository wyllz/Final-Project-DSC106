Writeup

What we've done so far

Our group found the Yelp Open Data, a dataset containing restaurants in 11 metro areas ranging from 2005 to 2022, and narrowed the content to 52,268 independent restaurants with a cuisine tag. Using Python, we counted how many similar-cuisine rivals sit within a 200m radius, then exported small JSON files that our D3 can load. We hypothesized that restaurants with nearby competitors hurt an individual restaurant’s survival; however, after analyzing, we discovered a twist. Yelp keeps closed restaurants in the data, so many “rivals” are not currently open, and they are really just “ghosts” (earlier restaurants that failed in the same spot and never competed at all). To fix the extraneous features, we used each restaurant’s first and last review dates to count only rivals that were open at the same time. With that correction, the crowding penalty shrinks from 19 percentage points to about 2. We built two interactive visualizations around this finding. The first asks readers to draw their guess before revealing the data and "busting the ghosts," with an animated explainer of what a ghost is. The second is a game where readers decide whether a new restaurant will survive in a storefront where others have already failed.

What will be most challenging, and why

The most challenging part will be properly explaining the “ghost” idea clearly. Our main findings depend on a data artifact that most readers would never have thought about, so if the explanation is unclear, the whole story ends up being confusing. We also need to keep our comparisons honest. For example, the ghost-free line follows a different group of restaurants and some of its columns are small, so we have to show uncertainty without overwhelming the reader. Another challenge is the page design: we want a scrolling layout where a character walks down a path past "plazas" for each visualization, which adds animation and layout work on top of the charts. Finally, Yelp's terms limit what we can publish, so we have to decide which data to keep aggregated and which details, like business names, are safe to show on a public site.

Source: Yelp Open Dataset (Jan 2022 snapshot). Independent restaurant = name with fewer than 5 locations in the data; rival = same primary cuisine. Survival = still open in Jan 2022. These are correlations, not causal effects.
