Crowd Cuisine Competition: do rivals really kill restaurants?

Restaurants of the same cuisine love to cluster, but common wisdom says a competitor next door is bad for business. Three stops through 52,268 restaurants from the Yelp Open Dataset.

Ghost Town: Philadelphia's restaurants through time

Press play to watch restaurants open (blue) and leave ghosts behind when they close (orange rings). Click any restaurant to see its same-cuisine "rivals": solid lines were open at the same time, dashed ghosts never were. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan.
